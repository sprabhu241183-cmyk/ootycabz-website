Important Ooty Travel Information

Ooty is a hill station and traffic can be heavier during weekends, holidays and peak tourist periods. Weather can also change quickly. Keep comfortable footwear, a light jacket and an umbrella when needed.

Some attractions may have separate entry tickets, parking charges or activity fees. These are not included in the taxi service unless specifically mentioned in your quotation.

Ghat road AC: During the hill-road section, vehicle AC may be switched OFF temporarily when required for vehicle safety, engine performance and safe hill driving.
