Travel better

Ooty Travel Tips

☂️ Carry Rain Protection

Ooty weather can change quickly, especially around the hills.

👟 Wear Comfortable Shoes

Several attractions involve walking, steps and uneven paths.

🧥 Carry a Light Jacket

Mornings and evenings can be cool in Ooty.

💧 Carry Reusable Water

Help keep the Nilgiris clean by reducing single-use plastic.

🕘 Start on Time

An early start gives you more comfortable sightseeing time.

🚗 Plan a Practical Route

Traffic, weather and attraction queues can change travel time.
