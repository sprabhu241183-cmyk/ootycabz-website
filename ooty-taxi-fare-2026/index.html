Why We Don't Publish Fixed Taxi Rates

Taxi fares can vary depending on the travel date, season, route, sightseeing plan, passenger count, luggage and vehicle type. For this reason, Ooty Cabz does not publish a fixed public rate card.

We will check your trip requirements and share the current applicable quotation before you confirm the booking.

✅ Clear & Transparent Quotations

When we send a quotation, we explain the applicable vehicle charges and inclusions clearly.

Attraction entry tickets, boating, safari fees, activities and personal expenses are separate unless specifically included in your quotation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fixed Ooty sightseeing fare?

We do not publish a fixed public rate because fares may vary by season, travel date, route and vehicle. Contact us for the current quotation.

Can the fare change between seasons?

Yes. Seasonal demand and operating conditions can affect the applicable fare.

Are attraction tickets included?

Usually, attraction entry tickets, boating, safari fees and activities are separate. Your quotation will clearly state the inclusions.

How do I book?

Send your trip details by WhatsApp or call Ooty Cabz. We will check availability and share the current fare.
