Plan Your Ooty Trip in 3 Days

If you are visiting Ooty for three days, a practical plan is to keep one full day for Ooty local sightseeing, one day for Coonoor and one day for Pykara. This gives you a good mix of gardens, viewpoints, tea attractions, lakes and Nilgiri mountain scenery without trying to cover too many places in one day.

Arrival and departure timings can change the route. Ooty Cabz can customise the itinerary according to your hotel, pickup point, travel time, passengers and preferred vehicle.

Day 1 – Ooty Local Sightseeing

Morning

Start around 9:00 AM from your hotel or Ooty pickup point.

Suggested route: Doddabetta Peak → Tea Factory & Tea Museum → Chocolate Factory.

Afternoon

Continue towards the central Ooty attractions.

Suggested route: Government Botanical Garden → Ooty Rose Garden → Ooty Lake & Boat House.

Tip: Keep some flexibility because traffic, weather, queues and walking time can affect sightseeing duration.

Day 2 – Coonoor Sightseeing

Morning to Afternoon

Start from Ooty and travel towards Coonoor after breakfast.

Suggested route: Sims Park → Tea Estate / Tea Factory → Lamb's Rock → Dolphin's Nose → selected viewpoints.

Travel tip: Coonoor roads are scenic but can become busy during weekends and holidays. A private taxi gives you flexibility to adjust the route based on time and weather.

Explore Coonoor Sightseeing Taxi →

Day 3 – Pykara Sightseeing

Full Day Pykara Route

Start around 9:00 AM from Ooty.

Suggested route: Kamaraj Sagar Dam → Pine Forest → Ninth Mile → Wenlock Downs → Pykara Waterfalls → Pykara Lake / Boat House, subject to time and attraction conditions.

Tip: Pykara is best planned as a separate sightseeing day so you can enjoy the scenic stops without rushing.

Alternative Ooty Itineraries

2 Days in Ooty

4 Days in Ooty

Day 1: Ooty local. Day 2: Coonoor. Day 3: Pykara. Day 4: Avalanche or a relaxed Ooty day, depending on weather and travel plans.

Ooty + Mudumalai

If wildlife is a priority, ask about a customised Ooty and Mudumalai plan. Mudumalai and Masinagudi require additional travel time, so they are usually better kept as a separate day.

Ooty Itinerary for Families, Couples & Groups

Families: Keep the schedule comfortable and allow extra time for children and older travellers.

Couples: Choose scenic viewpoints and tea attractions with a flexible private taxi plan.

Groups: Select a vehicle based on both passenger count and luggage. SUV, Innova, Innova Crysta and Tempo Traveller options can be considered according to group size.

What to Keep in Mind

  • Ooty is a hill station, so travel times can change with traffic and weather.
  • Start sightseeing early enough to avoid rushing later in the day.
  • Wear comfortable footwear because several attractions involve walking.
  • Carry a light jacket or rain protection when needed.
  • Attraction entry tickets, boating and activity charges are separate unless specifically included in your quotation.
  • During hill-road travel, vehicle AC may be switched OFF temporarily when required for safety, engine performance and safe driving.
