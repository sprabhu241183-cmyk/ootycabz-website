Coimbatore Airport to Ooty Travel Information

The journey from Coimbatore towards Ooty includes a mountain section with bends and hairpin roads. Travel time can vary with traffic, weather, road conditions, tourist season, airport delays and your exact destination in Ooty.

Ghat road AC information: Air conditioning is available during the plains journey. On the ghat road section, the AC may be switched OFF temporarily when required for vehicle safety, engine performance and safe hill driving.
