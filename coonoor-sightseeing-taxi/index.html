Coonoor tour from Ooty

A relaxed full-day Coonoor sightseeing route

Coonoor is known for its botanical garden, tea country and dramatic valley viewpoints. The main sightseeing route includes Sims Park, Lamb's Rock and Dolphin's Nose.

🌺 Sims Park

A botanical garden in Coonoor with unusual trees, shrubs and creepers. A popular stop for families and nature lovers.

🏔️ Lamb's Rock

A scenic viewpoint on the road towards Dolphin's Nose, with views towards the Coonoor hills and Coimbatore plains.

🐬 Dolphin's Nose

A major Coonoor viewpoint with grand valley scenery and views towards Catherine Falls when visibility is good.

🍃 Tea Estate Views

Enjoy scenic tea-country drives and photo stops around Coonoor, depending on road access, weather and available time.

🌄 Ketti Valley View

Add a scenic valley stop on the Ooty–Coonoor route when conditions and time allow.
